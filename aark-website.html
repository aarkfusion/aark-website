Our Story

Rooted in tradition,
designed for today

AARK was born from a love for India's rich textile heritage — and a belief that traditional craftsmanship should feel modern, wearable, and deeply meaningful.

How It Began

A thread
that connects
generations.

AARK started as a quiet passion project — sketches of silhouettes inspired by childhood memories of watching grandmothers drape sarees, mothers choose kurtis for temple visits, and children dressed in their finest for every festival.

We grew up around colour, texture, and the unmistakable beauty of Indian handloom and block prints. But we also noticed a gap — ethnic wear either felt too traditional or too trend-chasing. Nothing felt effortlessly right for the modern Indian woman who celebrates both worlds.

So we set out to change that. AARK (which means "sun" — a symbol of warmth, clarity, and energy) became our vehicle to bring thoughtful, beautifully crafted ethnic wear to families across India.

"We don't just make clothes — we create pieces that feel like home."

Founder, AARK

What We Stand For

Heritage Craft

Every print and pattern is rooted in India's centuries-old textile traditions. We honour Kalamkari, block prints, and handloom weaves.

Sustainable Choice

We source natural fabrics, minimise waste in production, and choose local over imported — for people and the planet.

✂️

Precision Stitching

Each piece is stitched in our own unit where quality is checked at every step. You receive clothes that fit and feel right.

Made with Love

Behind every garment is a skilled artisan who takes pride in their work. We believe craft is love, and it shows.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family First

From toddlers to grandmothers, AARK creates ethnic wear for the whole family to wear together with joy.

🎨

Thoughtful Design

Our designs balance tradition with wearability. Clothes that feel at home in a temple, a wedding, or a casual family outing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you shop.

Home/FAQ
🚚 Shipping
How long does delivery take? ▾
Orders are dispatched within 2–3 business days and typically delivered in 5–7 business days across India. Express shipping (2–3 days) is available at checkout.
Do you ship across India? ▾
Yes! We deliver to all major cities and most pin codes across India. Enter your pin code at checkout to confirm delivery availability.
Is Cash on Delivery (COD) available? ▾
Yes, COD is available for orders up to ₹2000 in most locations. A small COD convenience fee may apply. For higher value orders, we recommend prepaid payment for faster dispatch.
How do I track my order? ▾
Once dispatched, you'll receive a tracking number on WhatsApp/email. You can also contact us on WhatsApp for real-time updates on your order.
📏 Sizes & Fit
How do I know which size to order? ▾
Refer to our Size Chart page for detailed measurements. If you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up for kurtis and frocks for a comfortable fit. You can also send your exact measurements on WhatsApp for custom stitching.
Is custom stitching available? ▾
Yes! We offer custom stitching on most products. Share your chest, waist, hip measurements and kurti length on WhatsApp, and we'll stitch to your exact size. Custom orders take an additional 3–5 business days.
🔁 Returns & Exchange
Can I exchange for a different size? ▾
Yes! Size exchange is accepted within 7 days of delivery. The product must be unworn, unwashed, and with original tags. Contact us on WhatsApp to initiate an exchange. Return shipping is the customer's responsibility for size changes.
What if I receive a defective product? ▾
We're very sorry if this happens! Please send us a photo of the defect on WhatsApp within 48 hours of receiving the order. We'll replace or fully refund — no questions asked.
Do you accept returns? ▾
We accept returns only for defective or incorrect items. For size-related issues, we offer exchanges. Custom-stitched items are non-returnable unless defective.
💬 How to Order
How do I order from Instagram? ▾
DM us on Instagram @aark.fusion with the product name or image and your size. We'll guide you through the next steps and confirm availability.
How do I order on WhatsApp? ▾
Tap any WhatsApp button on the site or message us directly at +91 90001 07301. Share the product code, size, and delivery details and we'll help you complete the order.
What payment methods are accepted? ▾
We accept UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), debit/credit cards, net banking, and COD. For WhatsApp orders, we send a UPI payment link or QR code.

Still have questions?

We're happy to help. Reach us on WhatsApp for the fastest response.
